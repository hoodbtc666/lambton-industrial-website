About LIST

Industrial service. Technical credibility.

Lambton Industrial Services Limited is an Ontario-based industrial services company supporting manufacturing, construction, energy, oil and gas, petrochemical and infrastructure clients.

From planning to final turnover.

Operating from our industrial facility in Point Edward, Ontario, LIST provides shop and field services for piping systems, pressure equipment, structural components and custom-fabricated industrial products.

Our team supports projects from initial planning and material control through fabrication, welding, inspection, testing, documentation and final turnover. Services can be delivered as a complete project package or as individual technical and quality-control services.
