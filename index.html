About LIST

Industrial experience built around quality.

Lambton Industrial Services Limited (LIST) is a certified and trusted industrial service provider specializing in inspection, fabrication, and construction support across Canada’s major industries — including oil & gas, chemical processing, potash mining, pulp & paper, and power generation.

Our reputation is founded on safety, quality craftsmanship, and technical excellence. With decades of combined field experience, our team ensures every project is delivered safely, efficiently, and to the highest industry standards.

LIST supports clients as both a prime contractor and subcontractor, delivering focused services or turnkey support for shutdowns, maintenance and capital projects.

Piping Fabrication

From spool fabrication to field tie-in.

In-shop and field fabrication for carbon, stainless and alloy systems, including spool fabrication, fit-up and installation. Full QA/QC support includes weld mapping, NDE coordination, pressure testing and complete turnover packages.

Spool fabrication to ASME B31.1 / B31.3 with traceable MTRs
Field tie-ins, hot taps and small-bore / tubing work
Supports, spring cans and specialty items to detail
Hydrostatic / pneumatic pressure-test coordination
Shop blasting, coating and packaging for transport

Certifications & Licensing

Authorized for regulated industrial work.

LIST operates under recognized industry codes and authorizations, including TSSA requirements applicable to fabrication, installation and repair scopes.

ASME B31.3

Process Piping

Fabrication and repair of process systems for industrial facilities.

ASME B31.1

Power Piping

Installation and maintenance support for high-pressure power systems.

CSA B52

Refrigeration Piping

Construction and modification of cooling and refrigeration lines.

CSA B51

Boiler & Pressure Vessel

Fabrication and repair-in-kind of boilers, pressure vessels and associated piping.

These authorizations support compliance with Ontario’s Boilers and Pressure Vessels Regulation (O. Reg. 220/01) and Pressure Piping Regulation (O. Reg. 219/01).
