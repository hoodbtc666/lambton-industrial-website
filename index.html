Lambton Industrial Services Limited

Built for industrial work where quality matters.

LIST is an Ontario-based industrial services company supporting projects from planning and material control through fabrication, welding, inspection, testing, documentation and final turnover.

Our services can be delivered as a complete project package or as focused fabrication, inspection, technical and quality-control support.

One accountable industrial partner — from project planning to documented turnover.

Core capabilities

Multiple disciplines. One industrial partner.

Fabrication, welding, inspection and quality services organized around project requirements, applicable codes, client specifications and safe execution.

01▰

Industrial Fabrication

Process, power and refrigeration piping; pipe spools; carbon, stainless and aluminum fabrication; structural steel, skids, supports, tanks and custom industrial components.

02✦

Welding Services

Shop and field welding including pressure piping, structural, carbon steel, stainless and aluminum using SMAW, GTAW/TIG, GMAW/MIG, FCAW and MCAW processes.

03⌕

Inspection & NDE

Visual and weld inspection, magnetic particle and liquid penetrant testing support, dimensional inspection, pressure-test witnessing, field and vendor surveillance.

04✓

QA / QC

Quality plans, ITPs, material traceability, MTR review, NCR/CAPA, welding documentation, turnover packages, audits and regulatory coordination.

05◈

Corrosion & Coating

Condition assessments, coating inspection, surface-preparation verification, repair surveillance and asset-integrity support.

06◎

Calibration Support

Calibration scheduling, verification, certificate review, equipment-status control and coordination with ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratories.

Project questions

Start with the right information.

Providing the project scope, schedule and applicable requirements early helps LIST review opportunities efficiently.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send available drawings, specifications, scope of work, quantities, material requirements, applicable codes, requested schedule and any inspection or documentation requirements.

Can LIST support both shop and field work?

Yes. LIST provides shop and field services, with the final execution approach based on project requirements, location, scope and schedule.

Can I send large RFQ packages?

Yes. For large packages or multiple CAD files, email fahmad@lambtonservicesltd.com and reference the project name.

Where does LIST support projects?

LIST supports projects across Ontario and can support work throughout Canada subject to project requirements.
