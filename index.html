Core capabilities

One industrial partner. Multiple disciplines.

Fabrication, welding, inspection and quality services organized around project requirements, applicable codes, client specifications and safe execution.

01

Industrial Fabrication

Process, power and refrigeration piping; pipe spools; carbon, stainless and aluminum fabrication; structural steel, skids, supports, tanks and custom industrial components.

02

Welding Services

Shop and field welding including pressure piping, structural, carbon steel, stainless and aluminum using SMAW, GTAW/TIG, GMAW/MIG, FCAW and MCAW processes.

03

Inspection & NDE

Visual and weld inspection, magnetic particle and liquid penetrant testing support, dimensional inspection, pressure-test witnessing, field and vendor surveillance.

04

QA / QC

Quality plans, ITPs, material traceability, MTR review, NCR/CAPA, welding documentation, turnover packages, audits and regulatory coordination.

05

Corrosion & Coating

Condition assessments, coating inspection, surface-preparation verification, repair surveillance and asset-integrity support.

06

Calibration Support

Calibration scheduling, verification, certificate review, equipment-status control and coordination with ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratories.
