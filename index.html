About Us

Industrial support built around quality and delivery.

Lambton Industrial Services Limited (LIST) is an Ontario-based industrial services company providing fabrication, welding, inspection, equipment calibration, quality assurance and quality control services to industrial, manufacturing, construction, energy, oil and gas, petrochemical and infrastructure clients.

Operating from our industrial facility at 142 Kendall Street in Point Edward, LIST provides shop and field services for piping systems, pressure equipment, structural components and custom-fabricated industrial products. Our team supports projects from planning and material control through fabrication, welding, inspection, testing, documentation and final turnover.
